THE STORY BEHIND XPLOR

We couldn't find the app we needed.

So we built it ourselves — first for six mates, then for everyone who wanted the same thing.

It started with six mates, a weekend habit and one simple argument: who had actually completed the most?
01

First came the mountains.

We were spending weekends exploring caves, climbing summits and wild camping around the UK. Then we decided to complete every proper mountain summit we could find — the kind with a trig point, compass plate, cairn or marker at the top.

Every weekend became another two summits, another cave or another location added to the list.

02

Then the Notes app failed us.

Everyone had their own list. Different spellings. Different photos. Different ideas of what counted. There was no proper way to see who had completed what, where we had camped or which places the group still needed.

“Surely there must already be an app for this?”
03

There wasn't.

We searched for hours. There were route planners, navigation apps, weather apps and hiking databases. But nothing put all the locations together, let us tick them off and made it easy to compare progress with friends.

The exact app we wanted did not exist.

04

So we built it for ourselves.

The first version was just a basic private list for the six of us. Then we added maps. Caves. Wild camps. Progress. Profiles. Friends. Achievements.

Other people saw it and wanted access. Then friends of friends asked to join. The private tool started becoming something much bigger.

THE REST IS HISTORY

XPLOR is the platform we couldn't find anywhere else.

Built from a real problem, tested on real weekends and designed for everyone who wants to discover more of the UK.
